Which region brought in the most revenue?
SELECT region, SUM(total) AS revenue
FROM orders o
JOIN customers c ON c.id = o.customer_id
WHERE o.status = 'completed'
GROUP BY region
ORDER BY revenue DESC
North America leads with $482,000, 35% of total completed revenue.
